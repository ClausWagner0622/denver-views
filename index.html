2028–2031
Robo-taxi saturation
Waymo, Tesla FSD, and Zoox deploy at scale in Denver metro. Cost per mile drops below $0.40 — cheaper than car ownership. Commute burden collapses for suburban zones.
2032–2037
Commute-distance decoupling
Productive travel time means 45-min commutes are no longer a penalty. Research shows AVs could expand viable residential radii by 30–68%. Foothills zones reprice upward.
2038–2045
Parking land reclamation
Denver's 75,000+ parking spaces (est.) begin converting to residential and park. Urban core density rises. Suburban "view + nature" premium strengthens as contrast sharpens.
Sources: MIT urban sprawl modeling (68% city radius expansion), ScienceDirect AV land-use analysis, DWS Real Estate AV Impact Report 2025. AV adoption projected to reach 50% of road travel by 2040 (CNBC/Gartner).
Colorado NOW
NREL + 76 airports mapped
Colorado's NREL 2025 study assessed all 76 state airports for eVTOL readiness. Rocky Mountain Metro (RMMA) in Broomfield has a 30-yr master plan including eVTOL platforms in its Verve Innovation Park.
2028–2032
Vertiport network emerges
Morgan Stanley projects $1–5T global market by 2040. Joby Aviation reached FAA Stage 4 certification in Nov 2025. LA and Houston targeted for 2030 launch. Denver corridor follows 2–3 years later.
2033–2045
Property near vertiports reprices
Proximity to vertiports will act like proximity to metro stations — studies show 5–15% value uplift within 1km. Hilltop and Cherry Hills (flat rooftops, open air) are prime vertiport candidates.

2026–2032
Millennial peak buying
72.5M Millennials hit peak earning years. They accept 200–300 sqft less space for walkability and nature access (Urban Land Institute). Views + outdoor access = premium they'll pay for.
2030–2038
Gen Z reshapes demand
Gen Z = 30% of workforce by 2030. Climate-conscious, remote-work native. Prioritizes nature access, outdoor living, fiber internet. "18-hour city" appeal — Denver is a top-5 target city.
2030–2040
Boomer housing liberation
9M Boomer-owned homes vacate by 2035 (MBA). Large estate lots in Cherry Hills and Genesee enter market. Rare inventory at scale — well-priced today relative to future demand from younger buyers.
